## Prompts

### Stage 1

> Create one clean game character concept sheet with exactly three separate full-body zombie students, evenly spaced left to right, feet fully visible, on a plain warm light-gray studio background with no words, no labels, no border, no extra objects. Stage 1 concept: infected classroom survival. Keep all three as the same coherent family of simple school-uniform students, compact chibi proportions, oversized square-ish heads, low-poly faceted geometry built from connected beveled cuboid forms, opaque closed meshes, flat cel-shaded colors, simple readable red eyes, crisp consistent dark outer contour matching a low-poly toon game. Character A: slim ordinary student zombie in a rumpled white shirt, loosened tie and navy trousers, forward reaching arms, simple pursuer. Character B: noticeably stocky tank student with one tidy stack of books strapped securely to the back, broad continuous torso, slow heavy stance. Character C: forward-leaning charging student with a single chalk-dusted sleeve and clear running silhouette. The books and chalk are integrated wearable details, not floating props. Use restrained school colors and muted infection green skin. Friendly arcade horror, not realistic, no gore, no torn flesh, no weapons, no scenery, no shadows that obscure the silhouettes. Three distinct silhouettes with equal scale and neutral three-quarter view.

### Stage 2

> Create one clean game character concept sheet with exactly three separate full-body zombie school characters, evenly spaced left to right, feet fully visible, plain cool light-gray studio background, no words, labels, border, scenery or extra characters. Stage 2 concept: long school corridor, narrow lane pressure and readable projectile threat. Match one coherent family of compact stylized low-poly toon student/ staff zombies, faceted beveled cuboid-like forms, opaque connected bodies, flat clear colors and consistent dark game-outline. Left: very slim student runner in a loose uniform jacket, long forward stride, one bright corridor-pass lanyard as a silhouette accent. Center: bulky locker-room attendant zombie with a compact shoulder-high locker-door shield strapped flat to one arm and a key ring at the belt, heavy square silhouette. Right: ranged hall monitor zombie in a neat school staff vest and armband, holding a tightly rolled paper tube with one small paper dart visible, calm aiming pose, clearly different from runner and tank. Keep props attached and simple, no active projectile trails. Muted green skin, restrained navy/cream school clothing, small red eyes. Friendly arcade horror, no gore, no realistic flesh, no weapons beyond a paper dart, no corridor background. Three distinct silhouettes, equal height except the intentionally compact tank, clean three-quarter view.

### Stage 3

> Create a clean character concept sheet for a stylized 3D cartoon zombie school game: exactly three separate full-body zombie students, equal visual scale and evenly spaced, feet visible, plain cool light-gray background, no lettering, labels, border, gym environment or extra figures. Stage 3 is a school gymnasium full-scale arena. Keep a coherent low-poly game family: compact chibi bodies, large readable heads, faceted beveled block-like geometry, opaque connected limbs, flat toon colors, consistent dark outer contour, muted green skin and small red eyes. Left character: lean track-team runner in a simple navy-and-white PE uniform, one bright orange gym bib number shape with no readable text, athletic forward sprint pose. Center: broad basketball-club tank zombie in a padded navy practice vest with one securely strapped basketball-shaped elbow guard, square stable stance; keep the torso continuous, no floating gear. Right: PE-class charger zombie in a red track jacket, low forward lunge and whistle on a short lanyard, a single white court-stripe motif on the jacket. The three roles are visibly distinct through silhouettes, not oversized props. Arcade horror, energetic and readable at small mobile scale, no gore, no realistic anatomy, no background action, no text.

### Stage 4

> Create a clean low-poly toon character concept sheet with exactly three full-body infected school cafeteria characters spaced left to right, feet fully visible, equal scale, plain light warm-gray studio background, no text labels, no scenery, no extra objects. This is Stage 4: cafeteria escape, separate from any rooftop/old unused proposal. Use a single coherent compact chibi zombie game style: oversized readable heads, faceted beveled cuboid forms, connected opaque limbs, flat cel-shaded school clothing, consistent dark outer contour and muted green skin with small red eyes. Left: slim lunch-line student runner in a cream school shirt, navy pants, red cafeteria token clipped at chest, fast forward step. Center: stocky dish-room tank zombie in dark-blue apron, a single serving tray worn securely as a flat chest plate, broad square silhouette and planted stance. Right: cafeteria monitor ranged zombie in a white shirt, forest-green apron and simple yellow lunch-line armband, holding a rolled paper menu tube angled down, measured aiming stance; this is only a visual prop, not a new attack. Avoid chef hats and avoid resembling a boss chef. Props are attached and simple. Cartoon arcade horror, no gore, no realistic food splatter, no weapons, no kitchen/cafeteria background, make silhouettes distinct at small mobile size.
