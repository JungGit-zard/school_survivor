## 공통 프롬프트

`Use case: text-localization. Edit target: provided approved vertical four-panel comic. Change ONLY the four Korean speech bubble texts to the specified language verbatim, top to bottom. Bold natural local-language comic font neatly fits original white bubbles. Preserve exactly original art, color, framing, four panels, pink bob-haired girl, cubic green zombies, facial expressions, actions, props, backgrounds, and story sequence. No new captions or story changes; remove all Korean in speech bubbles; keep tall ratio and complete four panels.`
