# Stage 4 gray reflective ceramic floor art direction

- User reference translated directly: not plain white, but a cool light-gray kitchen ceramic surface with very fine gray grout and a soft ceiling-light gloss.
- The reflection is deliberately restrained so repeated UV tiles remain clean rather than becoming a bright repeated pattern.
- One 1254 x 1254 square source tile is used, not a pre-baked multi-tile sheet. At runtime it repeats as square 0.8 x 0.8 world-unit tiles: 36 columns by 40 rows across the 28.8 x 32 Stage 4 combat floor.
- The source is visually inspected after WebP conversion. No props, landmark, character, or UI art is part of this change.

## Targeted cleanup iteration

- Removed the visually rejected marble/vein/crack/rough-surface reading from the prior source.
- Final art is smooth, almost uniform, cool light-gray industrial hygiene ceramic. The only deliberate visual variation is the restrained rectangular ceiling-light gloss; grout remains thin and straight at the outer edges.

## WebP optimization

- Compression only: no artwork was regenerated or redrawn after visual approval.
- Post-compression visual inspection retained the smooth gray ceramic, narrow straight grout, and soft upper rectangular reflection without visible compression artifacts.
