Escape! zombie school — third-party VFX credits (Terry's VFX picks, 2026-11-15)
==============================================================================

Textures in this folder were downscaled/re-encoded for phones (see PROVENANCE.json).
Shader ports live in src/lib/vfxShaders.js and src/lib/vfxRibbonTrail.js.
Every effect was changed to drop its dark outline / ink rim (Terry: no black outline on any effect).
Not used anywhere: Cartoon Combat VFX sample (gmingassets) — its license forbids redistributing the files.
